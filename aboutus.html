OUR STORY

Who Are We?

We are Year 9 students working together on a Project-Based Learning (PBL) project about wildfire preparedness.

Through this project, we investigate the causes and consequences of wildfires and explore how people can reduce wildfire risks before they become dangerous.

We believe that understanding wildfire risks and taking responsible actions can help create safer communities and protect forests, wildlife, and natural resources.

WHAT DRIVES US

Our Purpose

Our project is guided by three important goals that shape what we want to achieve.

Our Vision

A future with fewer wildfires, healthier forests, protected wildlife, and safer communities for everyone.

Our Mission

To explore wildfire causes and risks while encouraging responsible prevention and preparedness actions.

Our Aim

To help reduce wildfire threats by promoting environmental protection and safer community practices.

OUR REASONS

Why We Created This Project

Wildfires can affect lives, ecosystems, and communities. We created this project to explore these challenges and possible solutions.

Protect Our Forests

Wildfires can destroy large areas of vegetation and natural habitats. We want to understand how forests can be protected.

Reduce Community Risks

Wildfires can threaten homes, buildings, and essential resources. We explore ways communities can prepare before fires occur.

Protect Wildlife

Animals can lose their habitats and food sources during wildfires. Protecting biodiversity is an important part of environmental conservation.

Understand Environmental Impacts

Wildfires can contribute to air pollution, soil damage, and ecosystem disruption. We want to investigate these environmental consequences.

WHAT WE WANT TO ACHIEVE

Our Project Objectives

These objectives guide our research and help us work towards a safer and more prepared future.

Investigate

Explore the main causes, risk factors, and consequences of wildfires.

Develop Solutions

Improve Safety

Protect Nature
