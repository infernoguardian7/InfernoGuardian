Learn More About Wildfires

Explore our learning resources and improve your wildfire awareness.

Wildfires can happen naturally or because of human activities. They can destroy forests, damage homes, harm wildlife, and affect people's health. Learning about their causes, effects, and prevention methods can help everyone make safer decisions. These materials are designed to make wildfire information easier to understand.

More Things to Learn

Remember these important facts about wildfire safety.

Common Causes

Wildfires may start from lightning, unattended campfires, burning rubbish, and discarded cigarettes.

Environmental Effects

Wildfires can destroy forests, damage habitats, kill wildlife, and pollute the air.

Fire Prevention

Never leave fires unattended. Keep flammable materials away from flames and follow fire safety rules.

Community Awareness

Share safety information with friends and family. Communities can work together to reduce wildfire risks.

Be Aware. Be Prepared. Stay Safe.

Wildfire prevention is everyone's responsibility. Learn the risks, follow safety guidelines, and help protect your community and the environment.
